# Add five homepage storytelling sections

## Goal
Extend the homepage with five visually distinct, conversion-focused sections that fit the clean orange, white, and black SaaS direction without making the page feel crowded.

## New sections
1. **Growth journey** — a clear discovery-to-conversion story showing how search, paid media, creative, and web work together.
2. **AI-powered advantage** — an image-led section explaining where AI improves research, targeting, content, and optimisation while keeping strategy human-led.
3. **Industry solutions** — a polished visual grid linking to the existing industry hubs and coaching-institute page.
4. **Proof gallery** — a clean showcase of existing SEO reports and campaign work using only real project assets and approved performance facts.
5. **Partnership principles** — a final confidence-building section covering clarity, connected execution, measurement, and continuous improvement before the blog and CTA.

## Design approach
- Continue the approved Sora/Manrope typography and orange, white, black, and cool-gray palette.
- Alternate light and dark full-width bands for rhythm, with restrained rounded cards and pill CTAs.
- Use existing service, portfolio, process, and report imagery; keep images fully visible and uncropped.
- Keep headings compact, layouts spacious, and mobile stacking deliberate.
- Avoid duplicate metrics, invented testimonials, generic icon-only grids, and decorative clutter.

## Placement
Insert the five sections after the existing core service stories and before the blog section, preserving the current CTA-then-FAQ order.

## Verification
Check desktop and mobile layouts for image visibility, spacing, text overflow, link behavior, and horizontal scrolling. Confirm typecheck and preview build are clean.
